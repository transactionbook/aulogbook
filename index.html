Questions?

The things drivers ask before downloading.

Will this drain my battery?
The GPS runs at 2-second intervals while you're driving — the same rate Google Maps uses for navigation. When you're parked, the background monitor drops to a low-power idle check that barely registers. Most drivers report no noticeable difference in daily battery life. The foreground service notification ("AuLogbook monitoring for movement") is how Android knows to keep it alive — it's not doing heavy work while you're stopped.
Does it keep tracking when Uber or Didi is open?
Yes. AuLogbook uses Android's foreground service to maintain GPS recording even when another app is in the foreground. You can switch to Uber, accept a trip, navigate with Google Maps, lock your screen — the kilometre counter keeps climbing. If your phone's battery optimisation is aggressive (common on Xiaomi, Oppo, Samsung), the app warns you and shows you exactly what to change in Settings.
Do I need to do anything at tax time?
Just open Reports and tap Export. The app builds a CSV, PDF or Excel workbook with every trip, its business/private classification, toll amounts, odometer readings and your business-use percentage. Your accountant can drop it straight into your return. The ATO logbook method requires 12 continuous weeks of records — the app tracks that automatically and tells you when you've met the threshold. Once established, the percentage is valid for 5 years.
What if I drive for both Uber and Didi?
The app tracks both. Tap "Uber" or "Didi" when a rider gets in, and tap "Dropoff" when they get out. Each platform trip is recorded separately with its own distance, tolls and time. The classification handles the deadhead legs between jobs automatically — driving between an Uber dropoff and a Didi pickup is business, not private.
Is my data sent anywhere?
No. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Everything — trips, receipts, photos, settings — stays in the app's private storage on your phone. The only time data leaves is when you explicitly export or back up (to a folder you choose, or via the share sheet to your email/cloud). You can back up automatically to Google Drive or Dropbox, and that backup is encrypted by your cloud provider — AuLogbook itself never transmits anything.
What happens to my data if I uninstall?
If you've set up Auto-Backup (free), your data survives in the backup folder you chose. Reinstall, link the same folder, and everything restores — trips, receipts, photos, vehicles and settings. If you never set up a backup, uninstalling deletes everything (Android clears app-private storage). That's why the backup prompt appears after your first few trips.
Why is there a 15 km/h threshold for auto-start?
The app waits until you're genuinely moving in a car — 15 km/h sustained for about 6 seconds — before starting a shift. That way walking to your car, waiting at lights or a brief GPS glitch won't trigger a false trip.
